PIZZA SAUCE

Reference #BOOM 0002
Prep Time10 MIN
Yield18 QT
Shelf Life6 DAY

Allergen Call Out

NONE OF THE TOP ALLERGENS PRESENT

LEAN Working

LEAN Working
  • Stage a 5-gallon Cambro, immersion blender, pizza paddle and 6-quart Cambros before starting.
  • Stage the 6-quart Cambros and PrepWizard labels before mixing so the sauce can move directly to packaging.
Before You Start
  • Scale the kosher salt, garlic base and basil before opening the tomatoes.
  • Keep one can of tomatoes separate from the remaining five to support the two-step tomato sequence.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
BIANCO DiNAPOLI CRUSHED TOMATOES6 - #10 CANS
KOSHER SALT120 g
GARLIC BASE [PREP ----]150 g
BASIL, LEAVES ONLY25 g

Method

  1. Place one can of tomatoes in a 5-gallon Cambro. Add the garlic base, kosher salt and basil. Puree thoroughly with an immersion blender.
  2. Add the remaining five cans of tomatoes to the Cambro. Stir thoroughly with the perforated mixing paddle for 60 seconds.

Package & Rotate

Transfer to 6-quart Cambros
