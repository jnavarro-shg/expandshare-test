WHIPPED RICOTTA

Reference #BOOM 0103
Prep Time10 MIN
Yield17 PORTION
Shelf Life5 DAYS

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: MILK

LEAN Working

LEAN Working
  • Stage a mixing bowl, whisk, tan #10 scoop and seventeen 8-ounce deli containers and lids before starting.
  • Arrange all deli containers before portioning to support an accurate 17-container count.
Before You Start
  • Scale all ingredients into the mixing bowl in production order to limit transfers and cleanup.
  • Stage lids, stickers and PrepWizard labels before mixing.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
RICOTTA3 LB
ROMANO, GRATED225 g
WATER175 g
KOSHER SALT5 g

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing bowl. Whisk thoroughly until evenly combined and smooth.
  2. Using a tan #10 scoop, portion into 17 eight-ounce deli containers.

Package & Rotate

Lid all the containers.
Label, date + rotate.
