TIRAMISU

Reference #SCC 0116
Prep Time45 MIN
Yield3 - 8X12 Pans
Shelf Life6 DAYS

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: MILK, EGG, WHEAT

LEAN Working

LEAN WorkingStage the 5 QT saucepan, three 5 QT stainless-steel mixing bowls, two wire whisks, rubber spatula, tabletop mixer and whisk attachments before starting.
Before You StartBring the mascarpone and cream cheese to room temperature. Keep the prepared coffee mixture cold until assembly.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Mascarpone Cream
Mascarpone Cheese1,233 g
Cream Cheese411 g
Heavy Whipping Cream846 g
Granulated Sugar420 g
Egg Yolks18 ea
Spiced Coffee
Rising Star Cold Brew Coffee840 g
Instant Espresso30 g
Ground Cinnamon12 g
Assembly
Lady Fingers117 each

Method

  1. Add 6 cups of water to the saucepan and bring the water to a slow boil.
  2. Mix the egg yolks and sugar in a mixing bowl and whisk together to incorporate.
  3. Place the mixing bowl over the saucepan and continue to whisk the yolks until the sugar is dissolved and the yolks are cooked and have reached 160°F.
  4. CRITICAL POINT: The cooked yolks should be thick, pale, fluffy, and completely free of lumps or any scrambled-egg consistency. When lifted with the whisk, the mixture should fall in a thick ribbon that briefly holds its shape before blending back in.
  5. Remove from the heat and continue whisking to cool, about 3 minutes.
  6. Combine the whipped yolks, mascarpone and cream cheese in the bowl of the tabletop mixer fitted with the whisk attachment. Mix, starting on low and gradually increasing to medium speed, until incorporated, homogeneous and free of lumps. Transfer to a stainless-steel mixing bowl and reserve.
  7. Add the heavy whipping cream to the bowl of a tabletop mixer fitted with a whisk attachment and whip to stiff peaks.
  8. With a rubber spatula, fold half of the whipped cream into the mascarpone-cream cheese-egg yolk mixture. Once fully incorporated, fold in the remaining whipped cream without overmixing. Overmixing will turn the mousse grainy. Reserve.
  9. Spread a thin layer of the cream cheese mousse at the bottom of each pan.
  10. In a stainless-steel mixing bowl, combine the cold brew coffee, instant espresso and cinnamon. Whisk thoroughly to incorporate and dissolve the instant espresso. Reserve cold.
  11. Dip, do not soak, the lady fingers in the spiced coffee mixture and layer the bottom of each pan.
  12. Spread a 1/2-inch layer of mousse over the dipped lady fingers.
  13. Add a second layer of dipped, not soaked, lady fingers.
  14. Add a second 1/2-inch layer of mousse and smooth evenly.

Package & Rotate

Transfer to the freezer uncovered for 3 hours.

Once frozen, label, date and rotate.

Store in the freezer.
