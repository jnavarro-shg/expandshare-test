TOUM

Reference #SCC 0110
Prep Time10 MIN
Yield10 QT

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: NONE OF THE TOP 9 ALLERGENS

LEAN Working

Stage EquipmentStage the large Vitamix (24C), 5-gallon bucket, large stick blender and Tiper Tie bags before starting.
Before You StartScale all ingredients before production; keep the water and sunflower oil cold until ready to use.
Set the emulsification station before transferring the garlic-lemon puree.
Stage packaging before emulsifying so finished Toum can move directly to portioning.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
GARLIC CLOVE, STEMS REMOVED1100 g
LEMON JUICE1100 g
KOSHER SALT100 g
XANTHAN GUM10 g
ICE COLD WATER300 g
SUNFLOWER OIL, COLD2 GAL

Method

  1. Combine the garlic, lemon juice and kosher salt in the cup of the large Vitamix blender (24C) and puree until smooth, starting on slow and turning to high speed . About 45 second
  2. Add the xanthan gum and ice-cold water to the cup of the blender, incorporate and activate the xanthan gum. About 15 seconds on high speed
  3. Transfer the lemon-garlic puree to a five-gallon bucket. And with a large stick blender, turned on high, create a vortex on the puree. Steadily stream in the cold sunflower oil, raising the stick blender as the oil becomes emulsified.
  4. Avoid the “pumping” of the blender but rather keep a steady wave/vortex to pour the oil into to avoid unnecessarily warming the oil.
  5. Once all the oil has been streamed in, give it a final mix to ensure all the oil is well mixed in.

Package & Rotate

Portion the Toum into Tiper Tie Bags, 3300 grams per bag
Label, date and rotate
