BOOMS MEATBALL

Reference #SCC 0114
Prep Time90 MIN
Yield
Shelf Life6 DAYS

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: EGG, MILK, WHEAT

LEAN Working

Before You Start
Stage a scale, three large stainless steel mixing bowls, 6 sheet pans lined with parchment paper, #12 Disher Scoop (green handle), and resting rack.
Prep
Whisk the eggs, and scale or portion all ingredients.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
GROUND BEEF20 lb
WHOLE EGGS, WHISKED20 ea
WHOLE MILK3000 g
GRATED PARMESAN CHEESE1730 g
ITALIAN BREAD CRUMB1730 g
GRANULATED GARLIC40 g
ONION POWDER16 g
DRIED MEDITERRANEAN OREGANO16 g
CRUSHED RED PEPPER FLAKES16 g
KOSHER SALT90 g
TOASTED & GROUND BLACK PEPPERCORN10 g

Method

  1. Combine the milk and breadcrumbs in a large stainless steel mixing bowl. Hydrate for five minutes.
  2. Add the whisked eggs, grated parmesan, granulated garlic, onion, salt, pepper to the hydrated breadcrumbs. Mix well.
  3. Add the ground beef to the floor mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. Add the breadcrumb mix to the mixing bowl and mix on speed one until just incorporated. DO NOT OVERMIX!
  4. Transfer the meatball mix to a shallow hotel pan(s) and chill for 20-40 minutes. Mix should be well incorporated, rested and chilled.
  5. With a #12 Disher Scoop (green handle), portion into 90-gram meatballs. Roll and tightly pack the meatballs.
  6. Place on a sheet pan lined with parchment paper.
  7. Transfer the meatballs to a Rational Oven set to 360°F at 60% humidity. Insert the thermometer probe into the middle sheet pan in a center meatball. Set cooking temperature to 165°F.
  8. Transfer to a fresh sheet pan lined with parchment paper + rest at room temperature for 15 minutes.
  9. Finish cooling the meatball according to the two-step cooling process for TCS foods; from 135°F to 70°F within 2 hours, then from 70°F to 41°F or below within 4 additional hours.
  10. Transfer the cooled meatball to the freezer to freeze thoroughly.

Package & Rotate

Portion the frozen meatballs into Tiper Tie bags; 24 meatballs per bag.
Label, date and rotate.
