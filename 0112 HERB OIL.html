HERB OIL

Reference #SCC 0112
Prep Time10 MIN
Yield
Shelf Life6 DAY

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: NONE OF THE TOP 9 ALLERGENS

LEAN Working

LEAN Working
  • Stage the large 1.5-gallon Vitamix, scale, stainless-steel mixing bowl, whisk and quart containers.
  • Stage containers, lids and labels before blending so the finished oil can move directly to packaging.
Before You Start
  • Scale and prep all ingredients before blending; keep the extra-virgin olive oil separate.
  • Load ingredients in production order to reduce handling and prevent omissions.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
SUNFLOWER SEED OIL2000 g
GARLIC CLOVES500 g
PARSLEY WITH STEMS, ROUGH CUT850 g
BASIL LEAVES350 g
ROSEMARY, PICKED15 g
THYME, PICKED15 g
NUTRITIONAL YEAST80 g
FRESH-GROUND BLACK PEPPER40 g
KOSHER SALT32 g
EXTRA-VIRGIN OLIVE OIL3000 g

Method

  1. Combine the sunflower oil, garlic and herbs in the cup of the large Vitamix blender (1.5 GAL). Gradually increase speed to high and puree until smooth. About 45 seconds on high.
  2. Add nutritional yeast, fresh-ground black pepper and kosher salt to the blender and puree until smooth.
  3. Transfer to a stainless-steel mixing bowl. Add the extra-virgin olive oil and whisk together until evenly combined.

Package & Rotate

Transfer to quart containers. Label, date + rotate.
