BOOM'S • SHG Culinary Recipe

Blush Sauce

Reference #BOOM 0106
Prep Time10 MIN
Yield5 C
Shelf Life6 DAYS

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: MILK

Before You Start

  • Gather tools & equipment: scale, mixing bowl, wire whisk and rubber spatula.
  • Scale and portion ingredients.
  • Print Prep Wizard labels (2).
  • Get funnel and 40 oz squeeze bottle ready to transfer the finished sauce, plus an 8 oz Delitainer for excess.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Pizza Sauce [BOOM 0002]660 g
Heavy whipping cream285 g
Ricotta Cheese, Galvani285 g
TILL Garlic Base30 g
Kosher Salt3 g

Method

  1. Combine all the ingredients in the mixing bowl. With the wire whisk, mix and incorporate thoroughly, ensuring the sauce has a consistent color throughout with no streaks.

Package & Rotate

With a funnel, fill the 40 oz squeeze bottle to just below the neck. Transfer the remaining sauce to an 8 oz Delitainer, using the rubber spatula to scrape all the sauce. Label, date and rotate.
