LEAN Working

Stage EquipmentLarge Vitamix (24C), 5-gallon bucket, large stick blender and Tiper Tie bags.
Before You StartScale all ingredients. Keep the water and sunflower oil cold. Set the emulsification station and stage packaging before emulsifying.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Garlic cloves, stems removed1,100 g
Lemon juice1,100 g
Kosher salt100 g
Xanthan gum10 g
Ice-cold water300 g
Sunflower oil, cold2 GAL

Method

  1. Add garlic, lemon juice and kosher salt to the large Vitamix. Begin on low and increase to high. Purée until smooth, approximately 45 seconds.
  2. Add xanthan gum and ice-cold water. Blend on high for approximately 15 seconds.
  3. Transfer the purée to a clean 5-gallon bucket.
  4. Place the large stick blender into the purée and run on high to create a strong vortex.
  5. While maintaining the vortex, steadily stream in the cold sunflower oil. Raise the stick blender gradually as the emulsion develops.
  6. Do not pump the stick blender up and down. Keep the mixture moving without unnecessary warming.
  7. Once all oil is incorporated, give the toum a final mix until the emulsion is uniform.

Package & Rotate

Portion 3,300 g per Tiper Tie bag.
Label, date and rotate.
