LEAN Working

  • Gather tools + equipment, scale and portion ingredients, stage Tiper Tie bags and scale to portion Comeback Sauce.

IngredientAmount

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in the stainless steel mixing bowl. With the wire whisk, mix and combine the ingredients thoroughly.

Package & Rotate

Portion the sauce to 2000 g in the Tiper Tie bags. Label, date + rotate.
