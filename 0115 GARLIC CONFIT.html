GARLIC CONFIT

Reference #SCC 0115
Prep Time15 MIN
Yield5 LB, DRAINED
Shelf Life21 DAYS

Allergen Call Out

CONTAINS: NONE OF THE TOP 9 ALLERGENS

LEAN Working

LEAN WorkingStage a scale, 2 inch stainless steel hotel pan, pint delitainers or small mixing bowl for weighing out ingredients, parchment paper, aluminum foil.
Before You StartScale and measure all ingredients.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
PEELED GARLIC CLOVES5 lb
KOSHER SALT30 g
DRIED OREGANO5 g
CRUSHED RED PEPPER5 g
SUNFLOWER OIL~ HALF GAL

Method

  1. Pre set the Rational to Roast, 0% humidity, 225°F and high fan.
  2. Combine the garlic, salt and spices in the hotel pan. With gloved hands mix the ingredients thoroughly and distribute seasonings evenly.
  3. Pour the oil into the pan, covering the garlic and creating a “head” of at least 1/8 of an inch.
  4. Cover with parchment paper, pressing down the parchment paper all the way around, creating a “seal” around the garlic.
  5. Cover with foil, making sure to close tightly around the edges of the pan. Transfer to the Rational and cook for 90 minutes.
CRITICAL POINTBe careful removing the pans from the oven. Hot oil will burn.
QUALITY CHECK POINTCheck the garlic to ensure that they're tender and cooked through. The garlic should be still whole and hold its shape but easily squeezed between two fingers and be easily spreadable, without being mushy. Add additional cooking time if necessary, alert the MOD if quality has been compromised and the product seems overcooked or mushy.
  1. Transfer the garlic to a perforated pan or lexan to drain, and cool.
  2. Cool according to the two-step cooling process for TCS foods; from 135°F to 70°F within 2 hours, then from 70°F to 41°F or below within 4 additional hours. Record the cooling and verify.

Package & Rotate

Portion the drained garlic into Tiper Tie bags, 5 lb per bag. Portion the oil to 3 quart per bag. Label, date and rotate. Reserve the oil.
