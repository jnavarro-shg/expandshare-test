Plate Build

  1. Stretch the 300 g dough ball evenly to 16 inches.
  2. Apply 150 g tomato evenly across the dough using the sauce pitcher estimate.
  3. Distribute 170 g mozzarella evenly using the 6 oz cheese scoop.
  4. Finish with 2 g Romano using the shaker.
  5. Bake at 595°F for 6 minutes.
  6. Cut into 6 equal pieces and serve as pictured.
